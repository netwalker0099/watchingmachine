Meet Watching Machine

Watching Machine is one addon for World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary (2.5.6) that does the work of a shelf full of others: raid buff and debuff checks, class tools like a swing timer, totem sets and a mana tick spark, gear sets, a loot browser, an instance lockout tracker, guild and chat tools, and more.

Everything starts from one dashboard: type /wmachine or click the minimap button. Every module can be switched on or off per character, and a guided setup explains each one the first time you log in.

Install and update Everyone

Get it from CurseForge (the CurseForge app keeps it up to date for you) or as a zip from the GitHub releases.

  1. Close the game, or be ready to restart it.
  2. Unzip into World of Warcraft\_anniversary_\Interface\AddOns, so you have an AddOns\WatchingMachine folder. When updating by hand, delete the old WatchingMachine folder first so no old files are left behind. Your settings live elsewhere (in WTF) and aren't touched.
  3. Start the game. On the character select screen, check AddOns lists Watching Machine as enabled.
New sound files need a full restartThe game only finds new sound files when it starts, so after an update that adds sounds, quit and restart rather than /reload. Everything else updates with a /reload.

First login: the setup Everyone

Every module starts off on a new character, and a welcome window asks how you'd like to set up:

ChoiceWhat it does
Guided setupA page per module: what it does and how it works, a switch to turn it on, and its main settings explained. Back and Next move through the pages; nothing is final. The best way to learn what's there.
Quick setupOne checklist of every module, with presets: Raiding, Guild officer, Everything, Nothing.
Copy another characterCopies one of your other characters' modules and settings.
LaterEverything stays off until you run /wmachine setup.
  • /wmachine setup (or the dashboard's Setup button) runs it again any time. What's on stays ticked and settings keep their values unless you change them. setup quick and setup guided jump straight to one.
  • What's new: when an update adds a module, your next login shows it on a New in Watching Machine page with its switch and settings. /wmachine setup new brings that page back.
  • Forget a character you've deleted or renamed: in the welcome window or /wmachine settings, pick it and click Forget its settings.

The dashboard Everyone

Open: /wmachine, left-click the minimap button, the Watching Machine button in the Escape menu, or Options → AddOns → Watching Machine.

One card per module, grouped into Raid, Guild & Social, Logging and Interface.

  • The switch on each card turns that module on or off for the character you're playing. An off module isn't running at all.
  • Open opens the module's window, with all its settings.
  • … (or right-click the card) opens its tools: things like "run a buff check now" or "show the swing timer".
  • Hover a card to read what the module does, how it's doing, and its slash command.
  • Fit sizes the dashboard to show every card; Hide inactive shows only the modules that are on. Drag the bottom-right corner to resize; the cards reflow into columns.

Per character Everyone

  • Which modules are on, and every module's settings, belong to the character you're on.
  • Shared between your characters: things that are about all of them: Your Characters, the instance and battleground logs, the PvP enemy list, world timers, Loot Browser lists and BiS marks, crit records and the mail and trade log.
  • Parts follow their module: the class tools, swing timer and mana tick spark are on only while Class Tools is.

Buff Check Raid

Open: /wmachine buffcheck

Checks everyone's buffs on every ready check, by itself: nothing to click.

  • You started the ready check: who's missing what goes to raid or party chat, so everyone knows what to fix.
  • Someone else started it: the same list is printed in your chat window only.
[WM] Buff check:
Missing Fortitude: Stabsworth, Pew
Missing Food Buff: Tankyboi

What it checks

Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, paladin blessings, Divine Spirit, Shadow Protection, food buffs, flasks, battle and guardian elixirs. Each one has a tick box in the window.

  • Class buffs are only expected when that class is in the group (no priest, no Fortitude complaint), and only for classes they apply to (rogues aren't told about Intellect).
  • A flask counts for both elixir slots. Offline and dead players are skipped.
  • PallyPower: if your paladins use it, each player is checked for the blessings they were assigned, and the report names the exact missing one.

The Group list

The window's Group section lists everyone with what they were missing at the last check and, with GearScore on, a GearScore column: a quick way to size up a group. Sort it by most missing, highest or lowest GearScore, or name. Inspect for GearScores fills in unknown scores.

TipRun Check Now (in the window, or the card's … menu) checks the group any time and shows the result only to you.

Debuff Tracker Raid

Open: /wmachine debuff

A small display showing which important debuffs are on your target (the boss): green there, red missing, yellow a weaker version.

CategoryDebuffs (best first)
ArmorImproved Expose Armor, Expose Armor, Sunder Armor, Faerie Fire
Physical damageBlood Frenzy
Shadow / Fire damageShadow Weaving, Improved Scorch, Curse of the Elements
Spell hitMisery
Attack speed / powerThunder Clap; Demoralizing Shout / Roar, Curse of Weakness
HealingMortal Strike, Wound Poison, Aimed Shot
Hunter's MarkHunter's Mark
  • Knows your raid: it reads the classes and specs present and only tracks what your raid can actually put up (a debuff nobody can provide isn't shown as missing). Turn auto-detect off to choose by hand.
  • Raid alerts (off by default): after a few seconds without a tracked debuff on a boss, it says so in raid chat (or a raid warning with assist), with a cooldown between repeats.
  • Bosses only: big trash doesn't count, and on multi-boss fights it follows the mob the raid is actually hitting.
Is this a boss?Target it and type /wmachine whyboss to see how the tracker sees it. /wmachine exclude stops a creature ever counting as a boss (unexclude to undo).

Aura Range Raid

Open: /wmachine range

Tells you the moment you step out of range of a party aura or totem: Moonkin Aura, Leader of the Pack, Trueshot Aura, Tree of Life, paladin auras, Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Wrath of Air, Totem of Wrath, Mana Spring and more.

  • A movable warning lists exactly what you walked away from, with an optional sound, and clears as soon as you're back.
  • No false alarms when you're dead, when the moonkin or shaman is dead or gone, on loading screens or after leaving the group.
  • Per-aura tick boxes, a sound tick, a lock, and Test to see the warning.

Hazard Alerts Raid · GTFO-style

Open: /wmachine gtfo

A sound the instant you're standing in something that hurts: void zones, fire, poison clouds, cleaves, lava, slime, drowning and fatigue. About 265 hazards in TBC raids and dungeons.

AlertMeans
High damageMove now.
Low damageYou're taking damage you could avoid.
FailYou failed a mechanic.
Friendly fireYour own spell is hurting your group.
  • Tanks (Righteous Fury, Bear Form, Defensive Stance, or marked main tank) hear the tank version, or nothing where standing in it is the job.
  • Tiny hits and old content far below your level stay quiet.
  • Pick a sound per alert and the channel, with Test buttons. /wmachine gtfo mute <spell ID> silences one hazard you don't care about (unmute to undo). The window lists your recent alerts.

GearScore Raid

Open: /wmachine gs (or /gs)

A gear score and average item level for you and everyone you mouse over, with the same numbers as GearScoreTBCClassic, so the scores people quote match.

  • Player tooltips: "GearScore: 1650 iLvl: 112". Players you mouse over are inspected by themselves: out of combat, close by, one at a time, and not in towns unless you tick … in towns and inns too.
  • Item tooltips: the item's score, and with Item tooltip details its enchant and gem bonus and what equipping it would change for you.
  • Character and inspect frames show the score. /wmachine gs unlock to drag it, lock to save.
  • Group list: everyone in your group, highest first. Scan group inspects whoever's missing; Send to group posts the list.
Another GearScore addon installed?Its tooltips are left to it; the group list still works.

ArmorySnap Raid

Open: /wmachine armory (or /as)

Quietly snapshots every raid member's gear (with enchants and gems) and talents while you raid, so you can look back at who wore what.

  • A full 25-man is captured in 15 to 20 seconds when everyone's in range; anyone out of range is retried.
  • Browse: pick a snapshot, pick a player, and see their gear laid out like the character sheet. Shift-click to link an item.
  • GearScore for every member and the raid's average.
  • /as snap [label] takes one by hand. Scanning pauses in combat and carries on after.
Already use the standalone ArmorySnap addon?Watching Machine reads the same saved data. If both are enabled, the module stands aside and says so: disable one.

Gear Sets Raid · ItemRack-style

Open: /wmachine gear

Save what you're wearing as a set and put it back on in one click, a key, a macro, or by itself.

Make a set

  1. Put on the gear, open Gear Sets and click New set.
  2. The editor shows your character in a dressing room with the 19 slots around it. Click a slot to choose from everything you own that fits it (or to take it off); right-click a slot to leave it out of the set. Faded slots aren't part of the set, so whatever you're wearing there stays.
  3. Name it and click Save.

Put it on

  • Equip in the window, /wmachine gear equip Tank in a macro, or a key (below).
  • Undo last swap puts back what you wore before.
  • In combat only the weapons change; the rest goes on when combat ends. It waits out casts and death too, and checks the result at the end, saying what didn't go on and why.

Automatic swaps

In a set's editor:

  • Talent spec: pick Spec 1 or 2 and tick Equip it when I switch to that spec.
  • Form or stance: Bear Form, Cat Form, a warrior stance, Stealth… Taking that form puts the set on; a form with no set of its own puts back what you wore before.
  • Mounted, or in battlegrounds and arenas: what you wore comes back after.

Keys, scores and best in slot

  • Key binding: pick Gear set 1–10 in the editor, then bind it in Key Bindings.
  • GearScore: with GearScore on, each set shows its score.
  • Compare with BiS: pick a spec and, with the Loot Browser on, the set lists the items you've marked BiS for it that it doesn't have yet.

On the character sheet

  • Slot menu: point at a slot for a row of everything you own that fits; click one to swap it in (on hover, Alt + hover, or off).
  • A warning when a set's item isn't on you, saying if it's in your bank (with Your Characters on).

Loot Browser Raid · AtlasLoot-style

Open: /wmachine loot (or /wmachine atlas)

What every dungeon and raid boss drops, what each profession makes, reputation and PvP rewards, and collections: Classic and TBC.

  • Show: Dungeons & Raids, Crafting, Factions, PvP, Collections (badge vendors, mounts, pets, world epics, sets, events) or My Lists. It opens on the instance you're in.
  • Normal / Heroic switch for heroic loot. Set pages show one line per set, by default your class only.
  • Search: type three letters (or press Enter) to search every item, plus instances and bosses.
  • Items: hover for the tooltip, Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click to try it on.

My Lists and best in slot

  • Alt-click any item to add it to your list (the one you last opened).
  • Right-click an item to tick it into any list or mark it BiS for one of your specs.
  • Each list is sorted by slot and shows whether you already have the item. Lists are shared across your characters.
Where does this drop?Every item tooltip in the game (bags, chat links, the auction house) says where it's from: "Drops from: Attumen the Huntsman (Karazhan)", reputation rewards, PvP and crafting, and your BiS marks.

Attunements Raid

Open: /wmachine attune

Every raid attunement and key, step by step, for each of your characters: Karazhan, Nightbane, Serpentshrine, The Eye, Hyjal, Black Temple, the dungeon keys, the five heroic keys, Ogri'la, Netherwing and (optionally) Classic.

  • Steps are green done, yellow next, grey later, each with its zone. Hover a step for the quest, creature, item or reputation needed.
  • My characters shows every alt's progress on the selected one.
  • Survey guild asks guild members running Watching Machine for their progress.

World Timers Raid · World

Open: /wmachine timers · print them: /wmachine wb (or wb guild, wb party)

  • Layers: target A'dal, Nutral, Minalei or Innkeeper Haelthol in Shattrath to learn your layer (numbered like Nova World Buffs).
  • Terokkar towers: when the Spirit Towers reset, per layer, with a warning 10 minutes before.
  • Dailies: today's dungeon, heroic and PvP dailies.
  • Darkmoon Faire: where it is, when it opens and closes, and your Sayge fortune cooldown.
  • Classic world buffs and Songflowers, with warnings.
  • Map pins for the Faire, towers, dailies and Songflowers.
  • Timers from guild and group members running Nova World Buffs are merged in (it only listens).

Ogri'la Helper Raid · Ogri'la

Open: /wmachine ogri · options: /wmachine ogri options

Remembers the Apexis Relic (and Monument) colour sequence for you. It opens by itself when you start a relic.

  1. As the relic lights each colour, click it in the helper, or press G, Y, B, R.
  2. Touch the beams. The next colour is ringed and moves on by itself each time; a wrong beam steps it back.
  3. Left-click a colour to remove it, right-click to remove it and everything after; Clear starts again.

It also shows your Apexis Shards and Crystals and your longest sequence.

How Class Tools works Every class

Open: /wmachine class

Class Tools has a tab per class plus a General tab for everyone; your class opens first. Each tool is a tick box (on or off for this character), and each warning's sound is chosen right there on its class's tab.

The CHECK YOURSELF frame

All Class Tools warnings ("Fel Armor is missing", "Use a mana potion") appear in one movable frame that hides itself when nothing's wrong. At the bottom of the window: a sound on a new warning, lock it, reset its position, and Show example to put two test lines up so you can drag it where you want.

Warning sounds

Every warning can play its own sound: the shared warning sound, one of Watching Machine's sounds (Alarm, Chimes, Ding, Uh-oh, Glass, Potion, Rune…), one of the game's (raid warning, ready check, queue pop…), or None. Picking one plays it. Warning sounds play on (General tab) sets the channel: Master is heard even with game sound turned down.

General: consumables and mana Every class

  • Flask or elixirs, food buff, weapon oil or stone: warned about inside raids (25-man by default; tick for 10-mans too). Casters are told about wizard oil, melee about sharpening stones. /wmachine consumes shows what it sees.
  • Use a mana potion (mana users, in combat): once you're 3000 below max and a potion is ready, naming the potion.
  • Use a Dark / Demonic Rune: once you're 1500 below and no potion is ready. Not below 35% health.
  • Both thresholds are adjustable, and each warning has its own sound (Potion and Rune by default).

Swing timer Warrior · Rogue · Hunter · Ret · Enh

Show it to move it: /wmachine swing · reset: /wmachine swing reset · options: Class Tools → General

A bar per weapon counting to your next swing: for warriors, rogues, Retribution paladins and Enhancement shamans (read from your talents), and an Auto Shot bar for hunters.

Main1.0

The white spark sweeps left to right; the red end of the bar is your latency.

  • Follows TBC's rules: Heroic Strike and Cleave replace the swing, a finished cast restarts it, haste rescales it, a parry speeds it up.
  • Hunters: green while a Steady Shot is safe, red where it would clip the next shot, yellow for the wind-up when moving delays it.
  • Flurry charges (shamans and warriors) show on the main-hand bar.
  • Drag it to move, drag its corner to resize (or pick width and height), and Lock the swing timer when done.

Shamans and paladins get extra marks: see Shaman and Paladin.

Mana tick spark Every class with mana

Show it: /wmachine manatick · options: Class Tools → General

Mana comes back in ticks every 2 seconds. A white spark slides across your health bar and reaches the end as the next tick lands, so you can time a cast to finish just after a tick.

White: the next mana tick. Orange: the five-second rule after you spend mana.

  • Five-second rule: after you spend mana on a spell, an orange spark crosses the bar over the 5 seconds your spirit regen is stopped.
  • Where: your health bar (default), your mana bar, or a bar of its own. Works with Blizzard's and ElvUI's player frames.
  • A bar of its own too: a stand-alone bar as well, showing your mana with the same sparks. Shift-drag to move it; pick its width.
  • Hidden at full mana out of combat (an option). Potions and Innervate don't throw it off.

Flyout buttons Mage · Warlock · Shaman

The class buttons (portals, the warlock button, the totem button) open a flyout of spells. In Class Tools → General:

  • Flyout buttons open: on hover (default) or on click.
  • Dock flyout buttons together in a row or column that moves as one; Lock flyout buttons to stop dragging.
  • Each group's direction (up, right, down, left) can also be Hidden.
  • Nothing moves in combat; changes made in combat apply after.

Warlock Warlock

  • Armor check: warns when Fel Armor, Demon Armor or Demon Skin has fallen off.
  • Demonic Sacrifice and Soul Link: only when you've taken the talent and it makes sense (no nagging with a pet out by choice).
  • Soulstone timers: /wmachine soulstone lists who's stoned and for how long.
  • Warlock button: hover for your demons, armors, and stones & rituals (Soulwell, summoning, Healthstone, Soulstone), with your Soul Shard count on each.
  • Each warning's sound is on the Warlock tab.

Mage Mage

  • Armor check: warns when Molten, Mage, Ice or Frost Armor is missing (its sound is on the Mage tab).
  • Portal button: hover for every portal and teleport you know; click to cast. /wmachine portals lists them.
  • Mage utility button: mana gems, armors, and food & water (Ritual of Refreshment, Conjure Water and Food), best rank nearest the button.

Shaman Shaman

The totem button

  1. Hover the button and pick a set: Melee, Caster, Healer or Tremor to start with.
  2. Click (or press your Drop the next totem key) to drop the next totem: Air, Water, Earth, then Fire.
  3. It starts again from Air when your totems are gone, after combat, or on Ctrl-click.
  • Right-click the button (or /wmachine totems) to edit your four sets: each element's totem, Auto (the best you know) or None.
  • Shift-drag moves it. Totem timers above it show each totem you have down and its time left.

On the swing timer (Enhancement)

  • Weapon sync mark: a blue mark on the main-hand bar shows when to press your sync macro (/cleartarget, /targetlasttarget, /startattack) so the off hand lands just after the main hand. It turns green with SYNC NOW, and a line says In sync or Out of sync. Set Off hand lands after by and an optional SYNC NOW sound.
  • Windfury cooldown bar: Windfury Weapon's 3-second cooldown after each proc, green when ready.

These options are on the Shaman tab.

Paladin Paladin

  • Righteous Fury check for Protection paladins only.

Seal twisting (Retribution)

On the swing timer's main-hand bar:

Main1.6
  • Point of no return (gold line): the swing has one GCD left (1.5s cut by spell haste, at least 1s). It turns red once passed: anything on the GCD now means no twist before this swing.
  • Twist window (purple, the last 0.4s): cast Seal of Blood (or the Martyr) here so Seal of Command still lands. Length adjustable 0.3–0.5s.
  • Your seal: its icon just past the end of the bar, or no seal in red.

The options are on the Paladin tab.

Guild Invite Group leaders

Open: /wmachine ginvite

Guild members type inv (or your trigger word) in guild chat or a whisper, and get invited.

  • Whisper requests are checked against your guild list first. You need to lead or assist.

PvP Enemy Tracker World PvP

Open: /wmachine pvp

Chat Filter Everyone

Open: /wmachine filter

Hides chat spam by phrase before you ever see it.

  • Premade filters (off until ticked): GDKP, Guild Loot Run, Gear Carry. Add your own phrases.
  • Ignores case, colours and link brackets. Public channels by default; say, yell and whispers if you tick them. Your own messages are never hidden.
  • Runs before Keyword Monitor, so hidden spam never matches a keyword.

Keyword Monitor Everyone

Open: /wmachine keyword

Watches Trade, General, LookingForGroup and other channels for words you choose ("Kara", "enchanter"…), with a sound and chat alert, and keeps matches for 5 minutes without duplicates.

Services Parser Everyone

Open: /wmachine services

Sorts boost adverts from the services channel by dungeon (Classic and TBC), with a separate tab for summons and portals.

Whisper Logs (WCL Lookup) Everyone

Open: /wmachine wcl

Lists who whispered you, with a ready-made Warcraft Logs link for each (fresh.warcraftlogs.com, the right server and region, cross-realm too). Scan Raid/Party adds your whole group. Click to copy a link, right-click to remove.

Auto Logger Raiders

Open: /wmachine logger

  • Turns on chat logging at login and combat logging in raids (and 5-mans if you tick it).
  • Keeps Advanced Combat Logging on, which Warcraft Logs needs for complete uploads.

Mail & Trade Logger Everyone

Open: /wmachine mail

  • Logs gold and items from mail, trades and the auction house, per character, with filters (gold, items, AH sales and buys, expired).
  • Bulk mail pulling is logged once, even with Postal-style addons.
  • Gold trades print a clickable chat line, and each trade records which instance you were in.
[WM] Trade with Booster: received 500g, gave 2 item(s) (in The Deadmines)

Instance Tracker Everyone

Open: /wmachine instances · print lockouts: /wmachine lockouts

The game lets each account enter 5 instances an hour and 30 a day. This logs every dungeon and raid on all your characters, so you know how many you have left and when the next one frees up.

[WM] Entered The Slave Pens (3/5 this hour, 7/30 today). [X]
[WM] The Slave Pens: 24m 00s, 61 mobs, 18,300 XP (300/mob, 23% of a level), 45,750 XP/hour, 4 more runs like this to level 62, 12g 40s 5c looted
  • Entering prints your counts. Click the line to open the log; click the red X to delete that run if it shouldn't count.
  • Leaving prints the run's stats: time, mobs, XP, what share of a level it was, XP per hour, how many more runs like it to level, and gold. To your chat window, your group, or off.
  • Same instance, counted once: going back in after a corpse run or disconnect adds to the same run. A reset makes the next one new.
  • Hover a run in the log for the group, times, XP, gold and reputation; right-click to delete.
  • It tells you when you can enter a new instance again, and as leader, tells your group when you reset.

Battlegrounds & Arenas PvP

Open: /wmachine bgs

  • Every match: result, length, killing blows, honorable kills, deaths, honor, damage and healing.
  • Arenas: bracket, both teams and your rating change.
  • Totals: games, wins and win rate per battleground and bracket, for one character or all.

Your Characters Everyone

Open: /wmachine alts

Every character you play, on every realm, in one window, kept up to date as you play.

  • Level, rested XP (still building while you're logged out), gold (realm totals on the realm line), and when you last played it.
  • Hover for bags, durability, professions, class reagents, and a hunter's ammo and pet.
  • Click a character for their gear on a paper doll, with GearScore.
  • Bags, Bank, Attunements and Lockouts buttons on each row.
  • Find an item: search every character's gear, bags and bank (open each character's bank once).
  • Right-click a character to remove one you've deleted.

Crit Tracker Everyone

Open: /wmachine crits

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
A module does nothingCheck its card's switch on the dashboard: modules are on or off per character.
"… is off on this character"Turn it on from the dashboard, or run /wmachine setup.
A new sound plays nothingFully restart the game (new sound files aren't found by /reload).
Frame Mover doesn't move windowsBlizzMove (or MoveAnything) is enabled; Frame Mover leaves windows to it.
Can't log out with the Escape menuType /wmachine logoutcheck with the menu open and follow what it says. /wmachine menubutton off then /reload stops the addon touching the menu.
"The game blocked … and blamed Watching Machine"Update to the latest version, then send the output of /wmachine logoutcheck.
Something errors/wmachine errors shows the last errors: send them in a report.
Can't find the mana tick spark/wmachine manatick shows it for 10 seconds, on the bar it's set to.

Report problems on GitHub issues or the CurseForge page, with the output of /wmachine errors.
